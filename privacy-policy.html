PRIVACY / BOMBING BUILDINGS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Bombing Buildings does not require an account and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address or other personal information to play. The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show adverts and Google Play Billing to process the optional one-time Ad-Free purchase. Those services may process device, advertising and purchase-related information as described below.

1. Who we are

Bombing Buildings is developed and published by Just One More Game Studio, under the Retro Revival label. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use the Bombing Buildings Android app.

For privacy enquiries, please contact: justonemore.gamestudio@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect directly

Bombing Buildings does not require user accounts or registration. We do not directly ask you for your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photographs, precise location or other personal information.

The game stores some local game state on your device, including settings and information needed to remember whether the Ad-Free entitlement has been observed. This local storage is used to operate the game and is not a user account or a profile maintained by Just One More Game Studio.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Bombing Buildings uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display interstitial advertisements. Ads are normally shown after a number of completed attempts unless the player has purchased the Ad-Free option.

Because the Google Mobile Ads SDK is a third-party service, Google may process information such as device and app information, advertising identifiers or other identifiers, IP address and information about ad delivery and interaction. The information and processing can vary depending on the ad request, device settings, applicable privacy choices and Google's advertising technology.

Google may use information to deliver, measure, limit, personalise or otherwise support advertising, and to detect fraud and abuse, subject to Google's policies and the choices available to users.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob documentation.

4. Purchases — Google Play Billing

Bombing Buildings offers an optional one-time in-app purchase called Ad-Free. The purchase is processed through Google Play Billing.

We do not receive or store your payment card number, bank details or other payment credentials. Google Play handles payment processing. The game receives purchase information needed to verify the entitlement, such as the purchased product and purchase status, and uses that information to disable advertisements for the account that owns the purchase.

The purchase entitlement may also be cached locally on the device for normal operation, while Google Play remains the authoritative source used by the game when it queries existing purchases.

For more information about Google Play's handling of purchase information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics and other services

We do not currently integrate a separate analytics, social-login, account-management or user-tracking service into Bombing Buildings. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the relevant functionality is introduced.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information processed by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Billing is handled by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies. We only use those services for the functions described in this policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not maintain a user account or a personal database for Bombing Buildings players. Local game data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its app data through the Android device settings.

Information held by Google as part of advertising or Google Play services is subject to Google's own retention, deletion and account controls. Please use the relevant Google account or Google privacy controls for requests concerning information held by Google.

8. Children's privacy

Bombing Buildings is a general-audience arcade game and is not designed as a service for children under a particular age. The game does not require children to create an account or submit personal information. Advertising and privacy treatment are subject to the settings and requirements applied by Google and applicable law.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Data transmitted to third-party services is handled through those providers' secure systems and protocols. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will show when the policy was most recently revised.

11. Contact

If you have a question about this privacy policy or Bombing Buildings' privacy practices, contact justonemore.gamestudio@gmail.com.
